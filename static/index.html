    How to spot a scam

    • Urgent language: "Act now", "Final notice", "Account blocked"
    • Requests for OTP, PIN, password, or CVV
    • Suspicious or shortened links (bit.ly, .xyz, .top)
    • Too-good-to-be-true offers or prize winnings
    • Generic greetings like "Dear Customer"

    Common scam types in South Africa

    • Bank impersonation (ABSA, FNB, Capitec, Nedbank)
    • SASSA / government grant fraud
    • Fake parcel or delivery fees
    • Lottery & prize scams
    • Job offer scams
    • WhatsApp investment / crypto scams

    Safety tips

    • Never share your OTP or PIN, not even with your bank
    • Hover over links before clicking; verify the domain
    • Call the official company number, not one in the message
    • Don't act under pressure, scammers rely on urgency
    • When in doubt, delete and report
