Scam Detector

This tool provides risk analysis, not guarantees. Always verify important messages.

    How to Spot a Scam

    • Urgent language (Act now, immediately)
    • Requests for OTP, PIN, or passwords
    • Suspicious links (bit.ly, strange domains)
    • Too-good-to-be-true offers

    Common Scam Types

    • Bank account alerts
    • Fake delivery messages
    • Lottery or prize scams
    • Job offers that ask for money

    Safety Tips

    • Never share OTP or PIN
    • Verify links before clicking
    • Call official numbers, not message numbers
    • When unsure, don't act immediately
